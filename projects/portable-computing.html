Portable Computing Completed

Technical Presentation on Portable Devices

CLOIF Course

Project Overview

About the Project

A technical presentation created for the CLOIF course covering the topic of portable computing. The presentation explores the evolution, types, and technologies behind portable computing devices that have transformed how we work and communicate.

Project Goal

To research and present comprehensive information about portable computing devices, their history, current technologies, and their impact on modern computing and everyday life.

Presentation Format

The project was delivered as a PowerPoint presentation to the class, covering key concepts, device categories, and technical specifications of portable computing hardware.

Topics Covered

The presentation covers various aspects of portable computing, including:

• History and evolution of portable computers

• Types of portable devices (laptops, tablets, smartphones)

• Key hardware components and specifications

• Battery technology and power management

• Connectivity and wireless technologies

• Current trends and future developments
