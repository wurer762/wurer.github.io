Budget Planner Completed

PowerApps Budget Management Application

ITTAU Course

Project Overview

About the Project

A budget planning application built with Microsoft PowerApps that allows users to manage their finances by tracking their budget, recurring costs, and monthly expenses. The app integrates with Power Automate to automatically handle monthly cost resets.

Project Goal

To create a functional and user-friendly budget management tool using the Microsoft Power Platform, demonstrating proficiency in PowerApps development, data management, and Power Automate integration.

Technology Stack

Microsoft PowerApps

Low-code platform used to build the main application interface, including screens for budget management, cost tracking, and data visualization.

Power Automate

Used to create automated flows that handle monthly cost resets, deleting one-time monthly costs at the end of each month to keep the budget accurate.

Data Source

Excel/SharePoint-based data storage for persisting budget information, recurring costs, and monthly expenses across sessions.

Features

Budget Management

Set and track your total budget with a clear overview of remaining funds after all costs are calculated.

Recurring Costs

Add repeating costs that are automatically deducted from your budget every month, such as subscriptions, rent, or utilities.

Monthly Costs

Track one-time monthly expenses that are automatically cleared at the end of each month via Power Automate integration.

Search & Sort

Search for specific expenses and sort your cost list by different fields to quickly find what you're looking for.
